Funded BTW - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

1. Who we are

Funded BTW is a private tool run by an individual operator, contactable at levi.claude.admin@gmail.com. It is used to manage the operator's own social media accounts.

2. What we collect

  • Account identifiers (such as username and display name) of the accounts that are connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, Instagram/Meta and Google/YouTube, used only to act on the connected account.
  • Content the operator creates, schedules or publishes, and the status of publishing jobs.
  • Performance metrics for the operator's own posts that the platforms make available (for example views, likes, comments, shares, watch time, follower or subscriber counts).
  • Operational logs (errors, job history).

We do not collect data about viewers or other TikTok, Instagram or YouTube users beyond aggregate metrics the platforms return, and we do not buy data.

3. How we use it

To publish content to the connected accounts, to show the operator analytics, to learn which content performs best, and to keep the service reliable and secure. We do not use it for advertising and do not sell or share it with third parties.

4. Storage and security

Data is kept in access-controlled private storage behind an authenticated dashboard. Tokens are stored only on the server side as secrets, never in public code or the browser, and are not published on these pages.

5. Retention and deletion

Metrics and content records are kept while the integration is active. When an account is disconnected, its tokens are deleted. To request deletion of any data associated with an account, email levi.claude.admin@gmail.com; requests are handled within 30 days. You can also revoke access in the platform's own settings (TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites; Instagram: Settings, Apps and websites; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).

6. Children

The service is not directed at children.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date will change when it is.

8. Contact

levi.claude.admin@gmail.com. See also the Terms of Service.
