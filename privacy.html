# Funded BTW — Privacy Policy **Effective date:** October 24, 2026 ## 1. Overview Funded BTW is designed to help users manage social media content, publishing workflows, and available performance analytics. This policy explains how information may be accessed, used, and protected when you use the service. ## 2. Information We May Access Depending on the features you enable and the permissions you grant, Funded BTW may access: - Basic profile information from connected social media accounts, such as display names and account identifiers. - Content you choose to upload, schedule, or publish through the service. - Available post-performance information, such as views, engagement, audience growth, and watch-time metrics where supported by the platform. - Technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. The information actually collected depends on which integrations are implemented and authorized. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information may be used to: - Connect and identify your authorized social media accounts. - Prepare and manage content publishing workflows. - Publish or upload content when permitted and authorized. - Display available post analytics and account performance. - Improve reliability, diagnose errors, and protect the service. ## 4. Platform Permissions Funded BTW accesses third-party platforms through their authorized integrations. You can revoke permissions through the relevant platform settings. Revoking access may disable connected features. Each platform's own privacy policy and terms also apply to your use of that platform. ## 5. Storage and Security Reasonable technical and organizational measures will be used to protect information handled by the service. Authentication credentials and access tokens should be stored securely and must not be exposed in public repositories or shared publicly. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. ## 6. Sharing and Disclosure Funded BTW does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate enabled features, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect security and legal rights. The service should request only the permissions and information needed for its functions. ## 7. Retention and Deletion Information should be retained only as long as needed for the service's functions, security, or legal obligations. Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of Funded BTW through the service operator's designated contact method. Connected-platform permissions can also be revoked through platform settings. ## 8. Children's Privacy Funded BTW is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law or the relevant platform's age requirements. ## 9. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated as the service develops. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made. ## 10. Contact For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the service operator through the contact method provided with the Funded BTW service.
